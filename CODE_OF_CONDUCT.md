# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This project is a small tool maintained by people who do it for free. Technical
disagreement is expected and welcome; contempt is not.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good faith. Ask what someone was trying to achieve before telling them they
  did it wrong.
- Critique code, protocols and measurements — not people.
- Be precise with claims. "It flickers on my terminal" is useful; "this is obviously broken" is not.
  Say which terminal, which macOS version, which command line.
- Accept that "no" happens. A patch can be correct and still not fit (see the scope rules in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)); a rejection is not a judgement of you.
- Keep bug reports and reviews on topic. Long design debates are welcome in an issue, not in the
  middle of someone's typo fix.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory comments about who someone is — including but not limited to their ethnicity,
  nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or
  appearance.
- Sexual language or imagery in any project space.
- Publishing others' private information (addresses, real names, hostnames, screenshots of private
  screens) without permission.
- Deliberately derailing, spamming, or reopening a settled decision to wear people down.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any other space where you
are representing this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break this document, and may warn, mute or
block the accounts responsible — temporarily or permanently, at their discretion. Reports are
handled privately; a report made in good faith will never be held against the reporter.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers through GitHub (a direct message to the repository
owner, or an email address listed on their profile) rather than in a public thread. Security
problems go to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
